# Maintenance status — 20 September 2026

OpenARDP remains available at its tested technical baseline. The expansion pause established by
[F038](../specs/038-local-document-pilot-readiness/spec.md) remains in effect. Preserve the implementation and its
evidence; use the [existing document workflow](30_LOCAL_DOCUMENT_WORKFLOW.md) when it serves an actual task.
Necessary maintenance is permitted. This status does not promise active feature development or a maintenance schedule.

## Practical evaluation status

The [prospective human pilot](../specs/038-local-document-pilot-readiness/contracts/pilot-protocol.md) is prepared and
has not started. Its [templates](../pilots/local-document/v0.1.0/README.md) remain unfilled. Human effort, independent
human judgments and voluntary reuse required by that protocol have not been observed.

A separate private, agent-operated document exercise on 19 September completed 18 of 30 planned attempts: six paired
tasks with three tool paths. Twelve attempts were not started after a documented post-start dispatch stop. Two selected
questions lacked sufficient historical or explanatory material in the chosen sources. Missing attempts remain unknown;
source-selection gaps must not be attributed to a particular tool. Sources and original answers were retained, with
separately recorded source checks and limitations.

This was not the F038 human pilot and is not a completed, publicly reproducible benchmark. It establishes neither human
time savings, recurring demand, product superiority nor product inferiority. Private source documents, transcripts,
machine paths and comparative measurements are not part of this repository update. Later editorial source research
does not replace those frozen inputs, repair old attempts or rescore their answers.

There is no new positive pilot or release decision. The existing expansion pause and independent
[F015 release gate](../specs/015-benchmark-security-release-gate/spec.md) remain unchanged.

## Work while expansion is paused

- Complete actual document tasks with existing tools. OpenARDP does not have to accompany every task.
- Fix a verified defect when needed for concrete use, within the usual risk-proportionate workflow.
- Preserve the tested core, original evidence and historical validation records. Pausing does not mean deleting them.
- Do not begin GUI, cloud, provider, connector, export or contract-stabilization work merely because it is on a roadmap.
- Record a recurring obstacle only when it actually occurs: the task, its source versions, the obstacle, and the simple
  workflow or native parser cache already tried. A short existing issue or working note is sufficient; no monitoring
  service, new benchmark framework or speculative backlog is required.

## Conditions for reconsideration

Use the [existing roadmap rules](08_ROADMAP_AND_GOVERNANCE.md#current-expansion-pause) and the unchanged human-pilot
protocol. A concrete recurring workflow and a demonstrated advantage over both current work and a usable persistent
native-parser cache are required before expanding investment. A candidate improvement must address the demonstrated
need in one bounded work package; the existence of a timing anomaly alone is not a reason to optimize.

The F038 prerequisites, cumulative budgets, stop rules, quality/source gates and voluntary-repeat-use requirements remain
binding. Missing evidence is not zero cost or a favorable result. A personal limited-GO would still not establish
external adoption: the existing requirement of at least two independent repeat users applies to broader open-source
investment. Neither a private exercise nor a pilot changes F015 release readiness.
